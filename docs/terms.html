Reporting and blocking

If you see something that shouldn't be there, report it: open the photo, tap •••, then Report Photo. It's hidden from you right away, and the library's owner is asked to review it. You can also block anyone whose photos you don't want to see; they aren't told.

Library owners

The owner of a shared library decides who is in it, and is responsible for it. Owners review reported photos in the library's Edit screen and are expected to act on them promptly: remove what breaks these terms, and remove anyone who keeps adding it. The owner of a server decides who has an account on it.

Your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app, under More > Delete Account. Owners can remove you from a library, and a server's owner can remove your account, if you break these terms.
