Privacy Policy

FrameStation Photos · Effective October 14, 2026

FrameStation Photos is a client for a photo library that you host yourself, on your own Synology NAS. Your photos and videos go from your devices to that server and nowhere else.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer does not collect, store, or have access to your photos, videos, account details, location, or usage information. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting service, and no tracking of any kind.

Where your data goes

  • Photos and videos you choose to back up are uploaded directly to the server address you enter in the app: your own NAS. They are not sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • Sign-in details (your server address and credentials or invite code) stay on your device and are sent only to your server.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, your server uses Apple Push Notification service to tell the app when your library changes. Those messages carry no photo content.
  • Maps and places are shown with Apple's MapKit, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Image analysis used for albums and memories runs on your device.

Photo library access

The app asks for access to your Photos library so it can back it up to your server and save downloaded items back to Photos. You can change this at any time in Settings.

Shared libraries

If you join or create a shared library on your server, the people you share it with can see what is added to it. Who has access is controlled by the owner of the server, not by the developer.

Reports and blocks

If you report a photo in a shared library, your server records the report so the library's owner can review it. The owner isn't told who reported it. If you block someone, your server records that so it can hide what they add from you. Neither is sent to the developer.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: More, then Delete Account. Your server deletes your account, signs it out on every device, and removes your personal library and albums. Their files are erased from the NAS after 29 days. Photos you added to a shared library stay there for its other members, shown as added by a former member. The developer holds no copy of anything, so there is nothing else to delete. If you signed in with a Synology account, that account itself is managed by your NAS's administrator.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
