FrameStation Photos

Your family's photo library, on your own Synology NAS.

Getting started

FrameStation Photos connects to a FrameStation server running on your NAS. Setup instructions for the server are in the project README. Once it's running, open the app and enter your server address. Sign in with your DSM account, or use the invite code someone in your family sent you.

Common questions

The app says it can't reach my server. Check that your phone is on a network that can reach the NAS, and that the server address and port are correct.

Backup is paused. Check the backup settings: with Wi-Fi Only or Only While Charging turned on, backup waits until those are true. It also needs access to your Photos library.

Where are deleted photos? In Recently Deleted for 29 days, then they're removed for good.

Shared libraries

Reporting a photo. Open a photo someone else added to a shared library, tap •••, then Report Photo. It's hidden from you right away, and the library's owner is asked to review it without being told who reported it.

Blocking someone. Tap ••• on a photo they added and choose Block, or use the menu beside their name in a library's Edit. You won't see what they add to shared libraries or be notified about it, and they aren't told. Unblock them in More > Blocked People.

Reviewing reports. If you own a shared library, reported photos appear at the top of its Edit screen. Remove takes a photo out of the library for everyone; Keep leaves it.

Your account

Deleting your account. In the app, go to More > Delete Account. Your server deletes your account and signs it out everywhere, and your personal library and albums are removed. Photos you added to shared libraries stay with them. Nothing on your iPhone is deleted.
